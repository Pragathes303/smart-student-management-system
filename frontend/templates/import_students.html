Supported formats: CSV (UTF-8) or Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) workbooks up to 5MB.

Spreadsheet Format Requirements

Your file must have the column headers shown below (order is flexible):

Name,Register Number,Department,Year,Email,Marks
Rahul Nair,REG2024101,Computer Science,1,rahul.nair@example.edu,86.5
Pooja Sundaram,REG2024102,Information Technology,2,pooja.s@example.edu,91.0
Manoj Kumar,REG2024103,Data Science,1,manoj.k@example.edu,74.0
  • Register Number: Must be unique for every student.
  • Department: Must match one of the institution's active departments.
  • Year: Academic year (1, 2, 3, or 4). Defaults to 1 if omitted.
  • Marks: Numeric score between 0.00 and 100.00. Automatic Anna University grade is computed.
  • Email: Valid unique email address for student portal credential delivery.
