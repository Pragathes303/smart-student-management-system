Core Privacy Principles & Governance

Applicable to all students, academic staff, and system administrators.

Student records constitute sensitive Personal Data. Our institutional information systems are designed in accordance with global data protection frameworks (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, GDPR principles, and FERPA standards). We guarantee strict adherence to the fundamental principle of Data Minimization: collecting only what is strictly necessary, protecting it with industry-standard controls, and granting students complete control over their personal identity.

✔

1. Minimum-Data Principle

We collect only academic identifiers (Name, Register Number, Department, Institutional Email, Marks & Attendance). Optional contact points (Phone & Date of Birth) can be omitted or updated at will. We never collect biometric, racial, financial, or political data.

👥

2. Role Isolation & Zero IDOR

Students are strictly isolated and can exclusively inspect their own performance. Cross-student directory searches and mark queries are cryptographically blocked and rejected with HTTP 403 on server boundaries.

👁

3. Public Verification Privacy

Official transcript QR codes point to a high-entropy public verification token. The verification page confirms only degree authenticity and CGPA, never revealing phone numbers, home addresses, photos, or personal contact info.

♻

4. Right to Erasure & Anonymization

Upon graduation or departure, students can exercise their Right to be Forgotten. Administrators possess one-click capabilities to permanently purge or irreversibly anonymize all PII while retaining anonymous institutional metrics.

Detailed Data Processing Breakdown

Data Category Specific Fields Purpose of Processing Retention & Anonymization
Core Identity Full Name, Register Number Official student identification, grading, and degree conferral. Permanent or scrambled upon Right to Erasure request.
Academic Performance Department, Course Marks, Credits, GPA/CGPA Curricular evaluation, university semester accreditation. Retained anonymously for institutional cohort statistics.
Contact & Emergency Institutional Email, Optional Phone, Optional DOB Result alerts, password resets, official correspondence. Immediately scrubbed upon student anonymization or purge.
Biometric / Avatar Profile Photo (optional) Student portal personalization and ID verification. Physically deleted from local and cloud storage upon erasure.
🛡 Technical Safeguards in Effect
All network requests modifying state require anti-forgery CSRF tokens; sessions are governed by HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and HSTS headers; all database operations strictly use parameterized SQL; and administrator audit logs record all data mutations without storing plaintext user credentials.
